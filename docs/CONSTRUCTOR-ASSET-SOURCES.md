# Expanded constructor asset sources — 6 October 2026

This register supplements [constructor identity behaviour and coverage](CONSTRUCTOR-LOGOS.md).
The user authorised local downloads of copyrighted Wikipedia/official marks for
identification. This is **not** a copyright or trademark clearance. Wikipedia's
non-free-use rationale is specific to its own article/context and is not a
transferable licence for this application. Operator approval/permission or an
independently justified legal basis is needed before deployment; do not treat
these source downloads as commercially cleared. No affiliation is implied.

The table records exact downloaded URLs and observed source labels, not our
legal assessment of whether those labels are correct. Public-domain/CC labels
do not extinguish separately held trademark or underlying artwork rights.
Ferrari's Prancing Horse is copyrighted; it is not labelled PD-textlogo.
Official Red Bull/RB downloads have no verified unrestricted reuse grant.

## Technical/source transformations

Downloaded raster originals remain unchanged. `renault.png` is the static raster
render of the original `Renault_logo.svg` URL above (Sharp 0.35.4/libvips, density
384, 638×438 RGBA). The source embeds three PNG layers; retaining them inside SVG
would violate our validator. The full original artwork was rendered, not redrawn,
recoloured or stripped of layers. No new client dependency was installed.
Reviewed SVGs may omit non-rendering editor
metadata and inert DOCTYPE declarations, inline the exact static source CSS
declarations, and add an intrinsic-coordinate viewBox when absent. Source path
geometry and colours are retained; no marks are fabricated or AI-generated.
Scripts, event handlers, external references/URLs and embedded stylesheets are
rejected by the final asset validator. These changes do not grant reuse rights.

- `red-bull-2026.svg`: the official ORBR header SVG's third nested group
  contains the Red Bull wordmark and bull emblem. Retained its circle and final
  two groups (original child indexes 2–4), with viewBox `578 159 424 164`.
  Removed Oracle, the separator, Racing and wordmark paths. The original
  red/yellow/white bull artwork remains unchanged; no white wordmark is hidden
  against the neutral tile. This derivative is not a newly licensed asset.
- `rb.svg`: retained original root path indexes 5 and 14 (grey RB letters,
  bull/team artwork and tiny trademark element); viewBox `58 0 71 63`.
  Removed Visa and Cash App paths, retaining team artwork unchanged.
- Ferrari uses the complete French Wikipedia shield, not a sponsor lockup.
  No official Ferrari challenge was bypassed.
- `force-india.svg`: retained root path 0 (white underlay) and the first group's
  first two original orange/green paths. Removed Sahara wordmark/star and other
  lockup paths. Original painted bounds are x≈6–154, y≈6–119; viewBox
  `0 0 160 125` retains the constructor's existing F emblem, not a redraw.
- `racing-point.svg`: retained original first-group path indexes 7 and 8,
  the constructor's existing circular point emblem. Its measured source bounds
  are x≈335–375, y≈164–204; viewBox `334 163 42 42` includes the whole artwork.
  BWT, sponsored separator and small lockup lettering are removed. Original
  path geometry and `#05233d` colour remain unchanged; no new badge is invented.
- The 2005–2012 Red Bull Racing source is restricted to those reviewed dated
  records. For 2013–2025, the unchanged Red Bull corporate bull/wordmark is a
  season-neutral constructor-owner identity aid, not a season-exact racing
  lockup. It is not used as the separate RB team's identity. The 2026 team
  graphic is not backdated onto those older records.
